# PRD 01 — Product overview

## Purpose

A professional communication tool for Muhammad Fahmi, iOS Developer. It answers one question fast: **"How quickly can a relevant person understand his professional value and find evidence of it?"** Visual impressiveness is secondary.

## Readers

| Reader | Needs in the first visit |
|---|---|
| Recruiters (Jakarta banks, telcos, tech companies) | Title, years, production apps, contact, CV |
| Hiring / engineering managers | Real products shipped, ownership, architecture |
| Technical interviewers | Stack, engineering decisions, depth in case studies |
| International freelance clients (Upwork) | Trust, past clients' countries, how to hire |

## Principle

**Claim → Evidence → Optional depth.** Every claim (hero, skills) points to evidence (projects, experience), and evidence links to optional depth (case studies, CV).

## Requirements

**Must**
- Present him as an experienced professional; the Apple Developer Academy is shown as continued professional development, never as a junior signal.
- Show production work (BRImo, MyTelkomsel) and shipped apps (KoalaFit) above learning projects.
- Make contact and CV reachable from every page.
- Contain only true facts. Unknown details are visible placeholders.

**Should**
- Work equally well for an Indonesian recruiter and an overseas freelance client.

**Won't**
- Blog, CMS, contact form, login, analytics dashboards, or any backend.

## Acceptance criteria

- A reader can state role, years of experience, and two production apps within 30 seconds of landing.
- Contact is at most one click from any page.
